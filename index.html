About

Artist first.
Every medium.

For the last 15 years I've worked as an artist across just about every creative industry there is: on walls and canvas, behind the camera, in the studio, and inside the code that people tap and click every day.

My title in every one of those rooms has been the same. Whether the final result is a mural, a music video, a mastered track, a written piece or a web app, I approach it as an artist, with the finished experience as the center of focus. The tools change. The standard doesn't.

That range is the advantage. A brand shoot is sharper when the photographer also understands design. A website feels better when it's built by someone who thinks like a filmmaker about pacing. A record hits harder when the person mixing it also directs the video. Clients get one creative point of view carried all the way through, instead of a handful of vendors guessing at each other's intent.

I work with artists, small businesses, event hosts, couples, agencies and developers, on single projects and on long-term creative partnerships.
